Privacy Policy

Nothing personal is collected. Last updated 6 November 2026.

The short version: Luna Fish collects no personal information. There is no account, no advertising and no tracking. The game sends anonymous counts about how it is played (such as which levels are hard) so we can make it better; these contain nothing that could identify you or your device. The game's progress stays on your device, or in your own private iCloud.

What Luna Fish stores, and where

  • Game progress (level, coins, fish caught, boat and gear) is stored on your device.
  • iCloud. If iCloud is on, the same progress is copied to your private iCloud storage, provided by Apple and tied to your Apple Account, so it carries over to your other devices. The developer of Luna Fish cannot read it.
  • Settings (sound, music and the like) are stored on your device.

Anonymous gameplay counts

To see which levels are too hard, where players stop and whether the game runs smoothly, Luna Fish sends short gameplay events to our own server. An event is a fixed name with a few numbers or words from the game's own lists, for example "level 12 reached, 95 seconds, 2 fish got away".

  • What is sent: game events (levels, catches, purchases in the game's shop, settings switched, screens opened), how long a play session lasted, how smoothly the game ran, crash counts, and the app version, iOS version, language and device model.
  • What is never sent: no name, email, account, advertising identifier, device identifier, location, contacts, photos or anything typed by the player. There is no player ID: events from one person cannot be linked together or to a person.
  • No address: the server sits behind a proxy that removes the connecting IP address and keeps no access logs, so we never see where a request came from.
  • How we count returning players: the device itself remembers the day the game was installed and how many times it was opened, and adds those numbers to its events. They stay on the device and are not linked to you.
  • Where it goes: to our own self-hosted server running the open-source Aptabase software. The data is not sold, shared or used for advertising, and no other company receives it.

What Luna Fish does not do

  • No account or sign-up.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no personal profiles.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No advertising or third-party analytics companies. The only outside code is the open-source Aptabase library, which sends the anonymous counts above to our own server.
  • No chat. The only way out of the game is the optional race code, which a player can send with the device's own share sheet.
  • No selling or sharing of data with anyone.

Features that use system services

  • Purchases. The Deep Sea is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; Luna Fish never sees your payment details.
  • Game Center (optional). If the device is signed in to Game Center, badges and high scores are sent to Apple's Game Center under that player's Game Center profile. This is run by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; Luna Fish receives nothing about the player.

Racing a friend (optional)

  • A friend nearby. Two phones in the same room connect directly over Wi‑Fi or Bluetooth (Apple's Multipeer Connectivity), with no internet and no server. Each phone shows up as a sea creature's name, never the child's name or the device's name. The Local Network question appears only when a race is opened.
  • A friend far away. The race runs through Apple's Game Center. Racing a Game Center friend shows each player the other's Game Center name; with a race code, the two players see only each other's sea creature. Game Center is run by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.
  • Race codes. A race code is four numbers. A player may choose to send it with the device's share sheet, as a short message with a link to playlunafish.venavitalis.com that opens the game on that code. That page keeps no logs and collects nothing.
  • What the phones send each other. Only the race itself: where the magnets and fish are, catches, snips and the score. No chat, no photos, nothing typed. Nothing from a race is sent to us, apart from the anonymous gameplay counts above (such as a race's score).

Deleting your data

Settings › Start over clears the game's progress. Deleting the app removes its data from the device. Progress kept in iCloud can be removed in the device's Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Storage. Because the gameplay counts carry no identifier, they cannot be traced back to a player, so there is nothing personal in them to look up or delete.

Children

Luna Fish is suitable for young children. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children, and its gameplay counts cannot identify a child.
