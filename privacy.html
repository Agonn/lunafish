Privacy Policy

Made for children, so nothing is collected. Last updated 14 October 2026.

The short version: Luna Fish does not collect any data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The game's progress stays on your device, or in your own private iCloud.

What Luna Fish stores, and where

  • Game progress (level, coins, fish caught, boat and gear) is stored on your device.
  • iCloud. If iCloud is on, the same progress is copied to your private iCloud storage, provided by Apple and tied to your Apple Account, so it carries over to your other devices. The developer of Luna Fish cannot read it.
  • Settings (sound, music and the like) are stored on your device.

What Luna Fish does not do

  • No account or sign-up.
  • No analytics or usage tracking.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No third-party SDKs.
  • No chat, no sharing and no links out of the game.
  • No selling or sharing of data with anyone.

Features that use system services

  • Purchases. The Deep Sea is bought through Apple's App Store, behind a grown-ups' check. Apple handles the payment; Luna Fish never sees your payment details.
  • Game Center (optional). If the device is signed in to Game Center, badges and high scores are sent to Apple's Game Center under that player's Game Center profile. This is run by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; Luna Fish receives nothing about the player.

Deleting your data

Settings › Start over clears the game's progress. Deleting the app removes its data from the device. Progress kept in iCloud can be removed in the device's Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Storage.

Children

Luna Fish is made for children aged 5 to 10 and does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
