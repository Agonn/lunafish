Luna Fish

Magnet fishing for kids.

Luna Fish is a gentle fishing game for iPhone and iPad, made for children aged 5 to 10. Hold a finger in the water to send the magnet out, let go to reel the fish in. Catch colourful fish, fill your tank, upgrade your boat, meet Luna the moon fish, and later dive to the deep sea. No ads, no accounts, no links out of the game.

Common questions

Is Luna Fish free?
Yes. Levels 1 to 50 are free to play. One optional in-app purchase, The Deep Sea, opens levels 51 to 100. It's a one-time purchase, with no subscription, and Family Sharing is on.

Can my child buy things by accident?
No. Children only see an "Ask a grown-up" button. The price and the purchase sit behind a grown-ups' check, and the App Store asks for the account's own confirmation too.

I bought The Deep Sea but it isn't unlocked.
Open Settings › For grown-ups and tap Restore purchase, signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with.

Where is the progress saved?
On the device, and in your own private iCloud if iCloud is on, so a new iPhone or iPad can carry on where you left off. Luna Fish has no servers.

How do we start again from level 1?
Settings › Start over. A purchase of The Deep Sea is kept.

What is Game Center used for?
If you're signed in to Game Center, badges and high scores show there. It's optional and run by Apple; the game plays the same without it.
